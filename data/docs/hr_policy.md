# Sample HR Policy (fake data for practice)

Leave policy for interns: Interns get 1 casual leave per month. Unused casual leave does not carry over to the next month.

Leave policy for full-time employees: Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid leave per year, plus 10 public holidays. Up to 10 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Sick leave is separate and allows 12 days per year.

Working hours: The office works Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM. Employees may work from home up to two days per week with manager approval.

Learning budget: Each full-time employee gets an annual learning budget of 50,000 INR for courses and books. Interns are not eligible for the learning budget.

IT support: For laptop or access problems, email it-help@example.com. Critical issues are answered within 2 hours.
